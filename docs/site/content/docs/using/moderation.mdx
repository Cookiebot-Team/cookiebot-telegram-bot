title: Moderation
description: What the bot does on its own, without anyone asking
---

Most of Cookiebot's value is in what happens while nobody is watching. This is
the part that runs unprompted.

## Joiners

A new member is screened before they can do anything interesting. The captcha
is a challenge in the group; passing it is the end of the matter, and failing
it — or ignoring it until `captcha_timeout_seconds` runs out — removes them.

Removal is not a ban. Someone who genuinely missed it can come back and try
again, which matters because the most common captcha failure is a real person
who put their phone down.

## Sticker spam

Stickers are the cheapest way to make a group unusable: a client can send
dozens a second and each one is a separate message. `sticker_spam_limit`
(default 5) is how many in a row one member may send before the bot intervenes.

Counted per member and per run — a conversation in stickers between two people
is not spam, and is not treated as it.

## Media restrictions

`media_restrict_seconds` holds a new member back from posting images and files
for a set time after joining. Off by default, and the single most effective
setting against drive-by image spam, because that kind of account posts within
seconds of arriving or not at all.

## Group guardian

Watches for the patterns that mean a group is being attacked rather than
annoyed — mass joins, coordinated posting, known-bad actors — and acts on the
group rather than on the individual message.

## What the bot will not do

- **It does not moderate admins.** An admin posting stickers forever is a
  problem for the group's humans.
- **It does not read messages it is not addressed with**, unless privacy mode
  is turned off for the bot in BotFather. This is Telegram's rule, not the
  bot's, and it is why some features need privacy mode off to work at all.
- **It does not act across groups.** Somebody removed from one group arrives in
  the next with a clean slate. There is no shared reputation.

<Callout type="warn">
Every one of these needs the bot to be an admin with **delete** and **ban**
rights. Without them the bot can see a problem and do nothing about it, which
looks exactly like the bot being broken.
</Callout>
