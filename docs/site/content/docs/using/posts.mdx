Partnered communities advertise in each other's groups. The publisher is how
that happens without fifty admins copying an image around by hand: a post is
submitted once, approved once, and delivered on a schedule to the groups that
agreed to receive posts.

## Submitting a post

1. A member replies **`/publish`** to the channel post they want shared.
2. The bot's **owner approves it**. Nothing reaches any group before that — the
   approval press has to come from the approval chat, and one from anywhere
   else is ignored.
3. A **daily sweep** delivers approved posts to groups that accept them, spread
   out rather than all at once.

A group that has turned off receiving posts gets nothing, and the scheduled
post for it is dropped rather than queued forever.

## Being offered a post

When Telegram auto-forwards a linked channel's post into the group, the bot can
offer to put it into the publishing queue: an **accept** and a **decline**
button under the post. Turn that offer off and the bot says nothing at all when
those forwards arrive.

## The four settings

All four live in [`/config`](/docs/using/configure), and all four are per group.

| Setting | Default | What it decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Publisher Post** | off | Whether approved posts are delivered *into* this group. Meant for groups over ~50 members |
| **Publisher Ask** | on | Whether the bot offers to queue a channel post that lands here |
| **Max Posts** | `3` | How many posts the bot may deliver here per day |
| **Publisher Members Only** | off | Whether only channel members may submit |
| **Thread Posts** | unset | Which forum topic delivered posts land in, for groups with topics |

<Callout>
Two switches, two directions. **Publisher Post** is about what this group
*receives*; **Publisher Ask** is about what this group *offers to send*. A group
that wants to advertise but not be advertised in wants the second on and the
first off.
</Callout>
